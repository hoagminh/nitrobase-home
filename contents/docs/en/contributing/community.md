---
title: Community & Issues
description: How to report bugs, request features, submit templates, and join the SaaSBase community.
---

We welcome contributions from developers worldwide!

* **GitHub Issues:** Report bugs and submit feature requests on [GitHub](https://github.com/saasbase/saasbase/issues).
* **Discord Community:** Join our community to ask questions and share your deployments.
* **Contributing Templates:** Submit pull requests to add new templates to the `templates/` directory!
