---
title: Alerts & Notifications
description: Send automated alerts to Discord, Telegram, Slack, and Email channels for deployment events.
---

Configure notification channels under **Settings -> Notifications**:
* **Discord & Slack:** Webhook integrations.
* **Telegram:** Bot Token and Chat ID.
* **Email:** SMTP configuration.

Supported trigger events include deployment successes/failures, scheduled backup errors, and auto-update notifications.
